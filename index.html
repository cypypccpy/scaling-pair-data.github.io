Research Note

Scaling Pair Data for Post-Training

The hand is the instrument of instruments. — Leonardo da Vinci

长期以来，大家都认为人类数据的质量比真机数据差不少，因此只将其用作预训练。只利用人类数据、不用任何真机数据，一般认为难以训练出复杂灵巧操作模型。我们通过大规模数据预训练的世界模型，找到了一种新的 scaling 范式：scaling pair data，成功地解决了这个问题。

Human data has long been considered substantially lower quality than real-robot data, and therefore has mostly been used only for pre-training. Training complex dexterous manipulation models using human data alone, without any real-robot data, has generally been viewed as impractical. With a world model pre-trained at large scale, we identify a new scaling paradigm, scaling pair data, that makes human-only post-training possible.

Introduction

The Beginning of Human Data Post-Training

Human data has recently become a central object of study for many researchers. Most work, however, still focuses on using ego-centric human data for pre-training and then relying on real-robot data for post-training. These studies are valuable, but the use of human data for post-training remains underexplored.

This comes from a common assumption: human data is considered low quality and unsuitable for training real robots. The reason is the embodiment gap. Manipulation is also an extremely dynamic task, where even a small deviation can cause failure.

Chapter 1

Why Study Human-Data-Only Post-Training

长期以来，人类数据被认为只能用于预训练。大多数研究这方面的工作会先用人类数据进行大规模预训练，接着使用真机数据进行后训练来完成任务。但是，给我的感觉有点像这和当时做很多 Zero-Shot Sim2Real 工作的理由是一样的。很多时候做 Zero-Shot 而不是 Few-Shot 的原因，是为了验证仿真的有效性。如果纯用仿真数据训练出的模型效果不好，大概率这个数据质量或者仿真器参数是有问题的。

所以人类数据也是一样的，能直接训练出模型的人类数据，才是质量最高的人类数据。它能直接带来两种收益：一是在部署场景中可以让机器人看人类操作即能学会任务，而不需要再遥操作收集数据。想象一下，在你的家里，一个万能的、啥都能做的机器人是非常困难的；但是一个看一遍你怎么做然后就能学会的机器人，对于模型能力来说是更容易达到的，前提是可以直接用人类的数据来微调模型。

但是，第二个收益才是最重要的，就是提高人类数据预训练的质量。人类数据的治理是一个非常难的课题，纯粹看小时数几乎毫无意义，十万小时低质量数据，对预训练的作用可能还不如 1000 小时高质量的数据。而什么样的人类数据质量最高？毫无疑问，能直接训练出复杂灵巧操作模型的数据质量最高。因此，研究人类数据后训练对于数据治理本身来说也起到非常重要的指导意义。

Chapter 1

Why Study Human-Data-Only Post-Training

For a long time, human data has been viewed as useful only for pre-training. Most work in this direction first conducts large-scale pre-training on human data, and then uses real-robot data for post-training to solve tasks. To me, this resembles the motivation behind many Zero-Shot Sim2Real studies. A key reason to pursue zero-shot rather than few-shot transfer is to verify the validity of the simulator. If a model trained purely in simulation performs poorly, then the data quality or simulator parameters are probably wrong.

Human data should be viewed in the same way. Human data that can directly train a model is the highest-quality human data. It brings two benefits. First, in deployment scenarios, a robot could learn a task by watching a human perform it, without requiring new teleoperation data. A universal robot that can do everything in your home is extremely hard, but a robot that can watch you do something once and then learn it is a more reachable capability, provided that human data can be used directly for model fine-tuning.

The second benefit is even more important: improving the quality of human data used for pre-training. Human data governance is a difficult problem, and simply counting hours is almost meaningless. One hundred thousand hours of low-quality data may contribute less than one thousand hours of high-quality data. What kind of human data has the highest quality? Clearly, the kind that can directly train complex dexterous manipulation models. Therefore, studying human-data post-training is also an important guide for human data governance itself.

Chapter 2

How to Train with Human Data Only

虽然是少数，但是学术界也已经慢慢有相关的研究出现。和 sim2real gap 类似，只使用人类数据进行训练的 embodiment gap 也可以拆分成两个具体的 gap：

  • Visual Embodiment Gap 人类数据无论是戴着手套，还是裸手，视觉上都会和机器人推理时差得比较大。这里的差异不只是视频里人手和机器人像素上的差别，还包括相机参数、环境变化等。
  • Dynamic Embodiment Gap 人类数据是用人手对物体进行操作，action 通常是通过设备或者视觉算法来获得。它和直接的机器人数据会有三层误差：手部姿态估计的传感器或视觉估计误差；人手和机器人关节不一样的误差；以及人手物理参数和机器人物理参数的误差，例如手的摩擦力和机器人的摩擦力不一样。

所有纯人类数据训练方案基本都是围绕怎么解决上面两个问题出发的。大部分方案可以总结为对人类数据进行 real2sim 重建到仿真器中，通过轨迹优化或强化学习等方法解决 Dynamic Embodiment Gap，然后直接 sim2real。这套方案最早是很多 Sim2Real 工作的延伸，问题有两个：一是 real2sim 技术本身也比较难 scale up；二是还是会面临 sim2real gap 的问题。

还有的工作不走在仿真器中训练模型然后 sim2real 的路线，而是将仿真器用来做类似数据处理的方法来解决 Embodiment Gap。对于 Visual Embodiment Gap，一般的做法是先分割，再 inpainting，然后再把渲染的机器人贴上去。对于 Dynamic Embodiment Gap，类似上面的方法用轨迹优化或强化学习进行处理，最后得到更好的数据来训练一个 policy。

这套方案最主要的区别还是训的是一个 visuomotor 的 policy，但是同样会面临两个问题：real2sim 技术本身难 scale up；图像 inpainting 这一套视觉处理管线有天生缺陷，很难处理复杂关系，例如遮挡等。因此，这个方向基本长期只能做一些比较简单且定制化的任务，以致于很多人认为纯靠人类数据无法训练出操作比较复杂的模型。

我们团队研究这个方向非常久，基本上市面上的所有方法我们都尝试过。最后我们通过用世界模型代替仿真器的方式，通过十万小时规模的人类数据进行预训练，我们的 Psi-W0 可以代替上面方法中仿真器的作用，通过在仿真器里做强化学习的方式，将一条人手轨迹优化成可执行的机器人轨迹，完成人手数据到机器人数据的转换。

本质上这一套方案还是沿用了之前的思想，只不过用世界模型来代替了传统的仿真器，好处是上面的各种 sim2real 和难以 scale up 的问题都绕过去了。到这算是解决了一部分问题，但接下来这一套方案会比较重，又需要 Policy 和 World Model 相互 rollout，又需要强化学习做微调。

后来我在思考，这一套，包括之前的方法麻烦在于本质上是因为：对于一条已有的人类数据，我们需要凭空造出与其对应的机器人数据，所以才需要各种强化学习、轨迹优化之类的方法来减少 embodiment gap。但是反过来想，为什么不能从已有的机器人数据出发，造出对应的人类数据呢？机器人数据无论是 visual 还是 dynamic，都是可以直接用的。

如果我们能获得大量和真机数据匹配的人类数据，然后训练一个模型，输入是人手的数据，输出是匹配的机器人图像和 action，就可以直接做到纯使用人类数据训练模型。这种人手和机械手匹配的数据，我们叫 pair data。因此，继去年 scaling robot data、今年 scaling human data 后，我们提出了一种新的 scaling 范式：scaling pair data。

Chapter 2

How to Train with Human Data Only

Although still limited in number, related studies are gradually emerging in academia. Similar to the sim2real gap, the embodiment gap in human-only training can be decomposed into two concrete gaps:

  • Visual Embodiment Gap Human data, whether collected with gloves or bare hands, looks very different from the visual observations seen by a robot at inference time. The difference is not only the pixels of human hands versus robot hands, but also camera parameters, environment changes, and other visual factors.
  • Dynamic Embodiment Gap Human data is generated by human hands manipulating objects, and actions are usually obtained through devices or vision algorithms. Compared with direct robot data, it contains three layers of error: sensing or visual estimation error in hand pose, mismatch between human and robot joint structures, and mismatch between human and robot physical parameters, such as hand friction versus robot friction.

Almost all human-only training methods revolve around solving these two problems. Most approaches can be summarized as reconstructing human data into a simulator through real2sim, using trajectory optimization or reinforcement learning to address the Dynamic Embodiment Gap, and then performing sim2real transfer. This line of work originated from many Sim2Real methods, but it has two problems: real2sim itself is hard to scale, and the system still faces the sim2real gap.

Other work does not train the model inside simulation and then transfer it to the real world. Instead, it uses the simulator as a data-processing tool to reduce the Embodiment Gap. For the Visual Embodiment Gap, a common pipeline is segmentation, inpainting, and then compositing a rendered robot into the image. For the Dynamic Embodiment Gap, similar methods use trajectory optimization or reinforcement learning to process the action data, and then train a better policy.

The key difference is that this still trains a visuomotor policy. It faces the same two limitations: real2sim is difficult to scale, and image inpainting pipelines have inherent weaknesses, especially for complex relationships such as occlusion. As a result, this direction has long been limited to relatively simple and customized tasks, which is why many people believe human data alone cannot train models for complex manipulation.

Our team has studied this direction for a long time, and we have tried essentially every method available in the field. Eventually, by replacing the simulator with a world model and pre-training on human data at the 100,000-hour scale, our Psi-W0 can take the role of the simulator in the methods above. It can optimize a human-hand trajectory into an executable robot trajectory through reinforcement learning inside the world model, completing the conversion from human data to robot data.

Conceptually, this still follows the previous paradigm, except that the traditional simulator is replaced by a world model. The benefit is that it avoids many sim2real and scalability problems. This solves part of the problem, but the pipeline remains heavy: the Policy and World Model must roll out with each other, and reinforcement learning is still needed for fine-tuning.

Later, I started thinking from the opposite direction. The difficulty of these methods comes from one fact: given an existing human trajectory, we need to create its corresponding robot trajectory from scratch, so we need reinforcement learning or trajectory optimization to reduce the embodiment gap. But why not start from existing robot data and generate its corresponding human data instead? Robot data is directly usable in both visual and dynamic forms.

If we can obtain large amounts of human data paired with real-robot data, then train a model whose input is human-hand data and whose output is the matched robot image and action, we can train models using human data alone. We call this matched human-hand and robot-hand data pair data. Therefore, after scaling robot data last year and scaling human data this year, we propose a new scaling paradigm: scaling pair data.

Chapter 3

Scaling Pair Data

将人手和机器人的 pair data 用于模型训练不是一个新鲜话题。很多预训练相关的工作都说明，将一些人类和机器人做类似动作的数据挑出来针对性训练，对模型理解人类数据和机器人数据混合训练会有帮助。但是这部分大多数都是用类似 retrieval 的方法从大量数据中检索出最类似的动作，不是完全 pair 的数据。而使用类似图像 inpainting 再从仿真中贴图上去的这种数据，碰撞关系和复杂遮挡容易失真还在其次，最主要的是这种数据的 action 还是来自于人类数据，会存在 dynamic embodiment gap。

想通了上面的逆向思维后，我们逆向使用 Psi-W0，从真正的机器人数据出发逆向生成对应的人手数据，产生大量高质量的 pair data。

高质量机器手和人手配对视频。

有了这些数据之后，我们训练出了新的模型：Psi-W0-Editing，一个通用的 ACWM for 通用数据转换。它能做到将各种人手数据转换成 pair 的机器人数据，也可以将机器人数据转换成 pair 的人类数据。

验证这个模型的泛化很简单：我们真正录一段人类在操作的视频，然后使用 Psi-W0-Editing 转换成机器人数据，再在真实的机器人上面进行 replay，看是否能够完成任务。结果这个模型的泛化能力非常好。我们可以做到大家任意拿自己的手机、相机等录一段人手操作的视频，都可以将其转化为各种机器人数据。我们将 Psi-W0-Editing 模型挂在了 在线试用页面（链接待补充） 上，欢迎大家试用。

有了这个能力，对于一条人类数据，我们可以直接通过 Psi-W0-Editing 将其转成机器人的数据，不管是 visual 上还是 dynamic 上都是完全 align 的。然后拿这个数据进行训练，就实现了前面说的后训练只使用人类数据，不需要任何 real2sim、轨迹优化、强化学习这些操作。我们在真实落地场景、复杂泛化场景和高自由度手灵巧操作上都进行了验证。

Chapter 3

Scaling Pair Data

Using pair data between human hands and robot hands for model training is not a new topic. Many pre-training works have shown that selecting human and robot demonstrations with similar actions can help models understand mixed human and robot data. However, most of these methods use retrieval to find the most similar actions from large datasets; they are not truly paired data. Data generated through image inpainting and simulated robot compositing may suffer from distorted collision relationships and complex occlusions, but the more fundamental issue is that its actions still come from human data, so the dynamic embodiment gap remains.

Once we understood the reverse formulation above, we used Psi-W0 in reverse: starting from real robot data, we generate the corresponding human-hand data and produce large amounts of high-quality pair data.

High-quality paired videos of robot hands and human hands.

With this data, we trained a new model: Psi-W0-Editing, a general ACWM for data conversion. It can convert various kinds of human-hand data into paired robot data, and it can also convert robot data into paired human data.

Evaluating the model's generalization is straightforward: we record a real human manipulation video, convert it into robot data using Psi-W0-Editing, and then replay it on a real robot to see whether the task can be completed. The model generalizes very well. Anyone can record a human manipulation video with a phone or camera and convert it into different kinds of robot data. We have deployed the Psi-W0-Editing model on an online demo page (link to be added), and welcome everyone to try it.

With this capability, for any human data trajectory, we can directly convert it into robot data with Psi-W0-Editing. Both the visual side and the dynamic side are fully aligned. Training on this converted data realizes the human-only post-training setting described above, without any real2sim, trajectory optimization, or reinforcement learning. We have validated this in real deployment scenarios, complex generalization scenarios, and high-DoF dexterous hand manipulation.

Chapter 4

What We Can Learn

这只是刚刚开始。我们对 pair data 的研究始于如何只使用人类数据进行后训练，这一部分我们可以认为已经是 work 了的。但这不代表传统的 pose estimation 不重要，相反，作为 ACWM 的输入，相关人类数据 action 的精度仍然能起到非常重要的作用。

对比于那种直接将人类数据和机器人数据一股脑扔到模型里混训的方法，这种高质量 pair data 更像是显式地将机器人数据和人类数据做了次对齐。这就像不同机器人的 action space 有关节空间的，有笛卡尔坐标空间的，大家已经基本认为真正效果好的模型需要将 action space 统一到一个空间下面，而高质量的 pair data 也是起到一个类似的作用，因此对于预训练也会有帮助。

下一步，我们会将 pair data scaling 到预训练的规模，让模型真正学会如何理解人手数据和机器人数据之间的差异，这部分是下一代基础模型核心的一部分。同时，这也证明了只使用人类数据是能够训练出模型来完成复杂任务的。但是我们不止于此，我们的目标是将人类数据的质量拉齐到和真机数据一样，甚至超越真机数据。我们确信，高质量的 pair data 是沟通这两种数据之间的桥梁，而我们找到了一种高效 scaling 它的方法。我们将会将相关技术用于预训练和人类数据数据治理，真正解放出人类数据的能力！

Chapter 4

What We Can Learn

This is only the beginning. Our study of pair data began with the question of how to conduct post-training using human data alone, and we now believe this part is already working. But this does not mean traditional pose estimation is unimportant. On the contrary, as input to the ACWM, the accuracy of human-data actions still plays a critical role.

Compared with directly mixing human data and robot data in a model, high-quality pair data is more like explicitly aligning robot data and human data. This is similar to the action spaces of different robots: some are defined in joint space, while others are defined in Cartesian space. The field increasingly agrees that strong models need to unify action spaces into a shared representation. High-quality pair data plays a similar role, and therefore can also benefit pre-training.

Next, we will scale pair data to the pre-training level, allowing models to truly understand the differences between human-hand data and robot data. This will become a core component of the next generation of foundation models. At the same time, it proves that human data alone can train models capable of complex tasks. But our goal goes further: we want to bring the quality of human data up to the level of real-robot data, and eventually surpass it. We are confident that high-quality pair data is the bridge between the two data modalities, and we have found an efficient way to scale it. We will apply this technology to pre-training and human data governance, and truly unlock the potential of human data.
